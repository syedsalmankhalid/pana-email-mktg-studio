# Leads Folder Rules

These rules apply to this folder only.

1. Everyone in `leads.csv` is made up. They are practice people, not real customers.
2. Never add real people or real email addresses.
3. Every row needs a consent value. Do not leave it blank.
4. Never change the `@example.com` addresses. That domain can never receive mail, so no real person is ever emailed.
5. Keep the columns exactly as they are: `first_name,last_name,email,group,joined_on,consent`.
6. The three groups are `tea-curious`, `gift-buyers` and `daily-drinkers`.
