# Kettlebird Tea: Brand Voice

Kettlebird Tea is for everyday people who want to turn their daily cup into a relaxing ritual. They don't need to be tea experts. We sell fresh, small-batch loose-leaf blends with simple brewing guides. Every cup should taste vibrant, cozy and personal. We are the opposite of dusty, mass-produced supermarket tea bags.

## 1. Our voice in three words

**Warm. Calm. Friendly.**

We sound like a warm friend mixed with a calm guide. We love tea and enjoy sharing a cozy recommendation. We are never snobby and never salesy.

How it shows up:
- Conversational and casual. Use contractions.
- Short, easy-to-read sentences.
- Light, gentle humor. No loud jokes.
- Emojis sparingly: at most one or two cozy ones per email (🍵 or 🌿). Put them in subject lines or the sign-off. Never put one inside serious instructions.

## 2. Words we use

Everyday, sensory words that feel inviting and calm:

- steep
- cozy
- ritual
- small-batch
- fresh leaves
- unwind
- warm cup
- hand-picked
- your daily pause

## 3. Words we avoid

**Tea-snob jargon.** Never use: terroir, astringency, liquor, cultivar, sommelier.

**Hype and fake urgency.** Never use: ACT NOW, LAST CHANCE, mind-blowing, revolutionary, guaranteed, BUY TODAY.

## 4. Claims we must never make

**Health and medical.**
- Never say our teas cure, treat or prevent any illness.
- Never say they boost immunity, burn fat or "detox" the body.
- We may say how a tea feels, such as "calming" or "gentle before bed". We never make medical promises. These claims break FDA and FTC rules.

**Quality and superlatives.**
- Never say "best tea in the world", "100% pure" or "certified organic".
- The only exception is when the product brief explicitly says so.
- We can't prove these claims, and we can be held legally accountable for them.

**Delivery and pricing.**
- Never promise "guaranteed overnight delivery" or "always free shipping".
- Shipping times are estimates. Say so.
- The only discount we may mention is the official 20% waitlist launch offer. Never invent other discounts or discount codes. It breaks trust and margins.

## 5. How we sign off

Use one of these two closings:

> Warmly,
> The Kettlebird Tea Team 🍵

> Happy steeping,
> The Kettlebird Tea Team 🍵

- The email always comes from the brand, "The Kettlebird Tea Team". It never comes from a single named founder.
- The sign-off may end with one cozy emoji (🍵 or 🌿), but only if we haven't overused emojis earlier in the email.
