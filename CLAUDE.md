# Email Studio

This studio is for planning and writing email campaigns for a small business. The sample business is Kettlebird Tea, launching The Steep Club.

## Folders

- `brand/`: the business's voice, tone, audience and style notes. Read this before writing any email.
- `leads/`: practice contact lists. Use made-up `@example.com` addresses only.
- `campaigns/`: campaign plans and the email drafts for each campaign.
- `notes/`: research notes and decisions, such as which skills were chosen and why.
- `reference/`: the lab guide and its screenshot. Read-only background material.

## Rules

1. Write in plain English. Short sentences, no jargon, no filler.
2. Never send email to real people. Sending through Resend is allowed only to Resend's test addresses (ending in `@resend.dev`) and to the owner's own email address. Everything else stays a draft or plan. A guard will enforce this in the next step.
3. Ask before deleting anything. This covers files, folders and git history. Wait for a clear yes.
