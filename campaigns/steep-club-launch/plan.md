# The Steep Club Launch: Campaign Plan

Kettlebird Tea. Email only. Drafts and plans, nothing is sent.

## 1. Overview

- **Name:** The Steep Club Launch
- **Summary:** Invite our waitlist to join The Steep Club, our monthly tea box, over four weeks of email.
- **Primary goal:** 300 waitlist members start a Steep Club subscription between Monday 7 December 2026 and Sunday 3 January 2027.
- **Secondary goals:**
  - Get each of the three groups to click through to the Steep Club page.
  - Keep unsubscribes low by sending only four emails.

## 2. Audience

About 2,000 people on our waitlist, in three groups.

| Group | Who they are | What they want | What holds them back |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tea-curious beginners | New to loose-leaf tea | An easy start, simple steps | "I don't know how to brew it." |
| Gift buyers | Shopping for someone else | A gift that feels thoughtful | "Will it arrive in time? Will they like it?" |
| Daily tea drinkers | Already drink tea every day | Fresh blends, a better daily cup | "Do I need another tea subscription?" |

All three are waitlist members, so they already know us. They are at the decision stage.

Note: 300 out of about 2,000 is a 15% sign-up rate. That is a stretch goal. We will check progress after the first email and decide whether to adjust.

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:** The Steep Club brings fresh, small-batch tea to your door each month, so your daily cup becomes a cozy ritual.

**Supporting messages:**
1. **Easy to start.** Every box comes with simple brewing guides. No tea expertise needed.
2. **Fresh and small-batch.** Hand-picked leaves, blended in small batches. Not dusty supermarket tea bags.
3. **A gift that keeps going.** A new box each month for someone you care about.
4. **Your daily pause.** A warm cup to unwind with, made better by something new each month.

**Offer:** The official 20% waitlist launch offer is the only discount we mention. We invent no other discounts or codes.

**Proof points:** Use only facts we can back up from the product brief. No prices appear in this plan. Add them from the brief when the emails are written.

**Message by group:**
- Beginners: simple steps, no pressure.
- Gift buyers: thoughtful, easy to give. Shipping times are estimates, and we say so.
- Daily drinkers: fresh leaves and something new each month.

## 4. Channel

**Email only.** No paid ads.

- **Why:** The waitlist already opted in. Email is low cost and fits a small budget.
- **Effort:** Low to medium. Four emails, each written in three versions (one per group).
- **Tool:** Drafts only for now. If sending is ever set up, it goes to `@resend.dev` test addresses or the owner's own address.

## 5. Calendar

Four weeks, starting the first Monday of December.

| Week | Send date | Email | Audience group | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mon 7 Dec 2026 | 01 Doors are open | All three (one version each) | Announce The Steep Club and the 20% waitlist offer. Drive sign-ups. |
| 2 | Mon 14 Dec 2026 | 02 What's in the box | Lead: tea-curious beginners | Show what arrives and how easy brewing is. Answer "I don't know how." |
| 3 | Thu 17 Dec 2026 | 03 A gift to steep | Lead: gift buyers | Show The Steep Club as a gift. Give an honest shipping estimate. |
| 4 | Mon 28 Dec 2026 | 04 Your offer ends soon | Lead: daily tea drinkers | Remind everyone the waitlist offer ends. Plain, calm, no pressure. |

All four emails go to the whole waitlist. Each group gets its own version of the same email, written to what that group cares about. The "lead" group is the one whose worry the email speaks to first.

**Dependencies:**
- The Steep Club sign-up page must be live before email 01.
- The offer end date must be confirmed before email 04.
- Shipping estimates must be confirmed before email 03.

**Open slot:** Wed 23 Dec to Sun 27 Dec stays free. If email 03 gets strong clicks, a short follow-up can fit there.

## 7. Success metrics

- **Primary:** 300 subscriptions started by Sunday 3 January 2027. Tracked by sign-ups from the Steep Club page.
- **Secondary:**
  - Click rate on the sign-up link, per email and per group.
  - Sign-ups per group, to see which group responds best.
  - Unsubscribe rate per email.
  - Email delivery and bounce rate.
- **Targets for the secondary metrics:** None set yet. We have no past Kettlebird results, and we won't guess. Set them after email 01 is sent.
- **Note on open rates:** Many email apps load images automatically, which inflates open counts. Treat opens as a rough signal, and trust clicks and sign-ups.
- **Reporting:** A short check after each send, and a full review in the week after 3 January.

## 8. Budget

Small. Email only, no paid ads. Costs are time to write and review, plus the email tool the owner already uses. No budget figures were given, so none appear here.

## 9. Risks

| Risk | What we do |
|---|---|
| The 15% sign-up goal is too high. | Check after email 01. Adjust the goal or the follow-up plan if needed. |
| Gift buyers worry about holiday delivery. | Give an honest shipping estimate. Say that times are estimates. Never promise a delivery date. |
| Too many emails tire the list. | Keep it to four. Watch unsubscribes after each send. |
| The offer end date is unclear. | Confirm it with the owner before email 04 is written. |

## 10. Next steps and decisions needed

1. **Owner decides:** the end date of the 20% waitlist offer. A proposed date is Sunday 3 January 2027.
2. **Owner confirms:** shipping estimates for the gift email.
3. **Owner confirms:** the Steep Club sign-up page will be ready by 7 December.
4. Write the four emails in `campaigns/steep-club-launch/emails/`, following `brand/voice.md`.
5. Run a brand check on each draft before it goes anywhere.
